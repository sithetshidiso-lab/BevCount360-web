The Operational Rhythm

How BevCount360 works

A simple daily and monthly rhythm that gives you visibility without complexity.

1

Set up your products and suppliers

Load your stock catalogue, categories, pricing, and supplier details once. We help you do this in your first session — takes under an hour for most outlets.

2

Record every movement — daily

Sales (via POS or manual), incoming stock from suppliers, wastage, and transfers. The system builds your operational picture in real time as your staff logs activity.

3

Count and reconcile on schedule

Physical stock counts — done by your team or ours — are logged into the system. BevCount360 instantly calculates variance: expected stock vs actual stock. Any gap is visible, named, and documented.

4

See your operational report

Get a clear summary of what came in, what went out, what was sold, what's missing, and whether your cash matches your numbers. No guesswork. No spreadsheets. Just answers.
